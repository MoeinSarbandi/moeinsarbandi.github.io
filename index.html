Moein Sarbandi

Marie Skłodowska-Curie PhD Fellow in Automatic Control · Centrale Nantes · DENSE Doctoral Network · LS2N Research Laboratory

École Centrale Nantes

Nantes, France

Email: moein.sarbandi@ec-nantes.fr

I am an MSCA doctoral researcher in Automatic Control at the Laboratory of Digital Sciences of Nantes (LS2N) (CODEx group), École Centrale Nantes, funded by the DENSE Doctoral Network under Horizon Europe. My PhD research is supervised by Prof. Franck Plestan and Prof. Mohamed Assaad Hamida.

My research focuses on robust nonlinear control, data-driven control, and learning-enhanced control of uncertain dynamical systems, with applications to floating offshore wind turbine (FOWT) control. In particular, I study data-driven sliding-mode control, data informativity for control, super-twisting algorithms, adaptive integral sliding-mode control, and nonlinear observers, including wind-speed estimation. I use MATLAB/Simulink and OpenFAST for simulation and software-in-the-loop validation. In Wind Energy Science, my co-authors and I validated a sliding-mode approach to wind-speed estimation in OpenFAST and in a scaled experiment.

Research interests

  • Adaptive and robust nonlinear control: integral sliding-mode control, super-twisting algorithms, adaptive-gain methods, and CLF-QP
  • Data-driven control: Willems’ Fundamental Lemma, robust informativity using QMI/S-lemma tools, Koopman-operator methods, and DeePC
  • Learning-based control: online neural networks integrated with classical sliding-mode designs
  • Predictive control: model predictive control and data-enabled predictive control
  • Nonlinear observers: second-order sliding-mode observers for state, disturbance, wind-speed, and aerodynamic-parameter estimation
  • Applications: renewable-energy systems, especially floating offshore wind turbines

Research mobility and collaboration

I have undertaken DENSE research secondments on data-driven control at BTU Cottbus–Senftenberg and at the Sustainable Power Systems Lab at Cyprus University of Technology. I also collaborate with Prof. Antonella Ferrara’s group at the University of Pavia.

Academic service

I have completed 35+ peer reviews across 12 journals and review for control conferences, as detailed under academic activities.
