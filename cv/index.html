Professional Summary

PhD researcher at LS2N (CODEx group) / École Centrale de Nantes, funded by the DENSE Doctoral Network (Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions, Horizon Europe). Research spans sliding mode control, data-driven control, adaptive neural network control, and nonlinear observer design, applied to floating offshore wind turbines.
