Professional Summary

PhD researcher at LS2N (CODEx group), École Centrale Nantes, funded by the DENSE Doctoral Network under the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions. My research develops robust, data-driven, and learning-enhanced control methods for uncertain nonlinear systems, with applications to floating offshore wind turbines.

Academic Interests

Adaptive and Robust Nonlinear Control: Integral sliding-mode control, super-twisting, adaptive-gain methods, CLF-QP
Data-Driven Control: Willems' Fundamental Lemma, robust informativity, QMI/S-lemma, Koopman-operator methods
Learning-Based Control: Online neural networks integrated with classical sliding-mode design
Predictive Control: Model predictive control and data-enabled predictive control (DeePC)
Nonlinear Observer Design: Second-order sliding-mode observers for state and disturbance estimation
Applications: Renewable-energy systems, particularly floating offshore wind turbines
