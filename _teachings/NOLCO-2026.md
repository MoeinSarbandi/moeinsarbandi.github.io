## Course Overview

The Fall 2026 course and laboratory expands the [2025 NOLCO laboratory]({{ '/teachings/NOLCO/' | relative_url }}) into combined course and lab instruction. Topics include aerodynamic and PMSG electrical control, feedback linearization, sliding-mode control, and super-twisting algorithms for wind turbines.
