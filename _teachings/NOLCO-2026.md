## Course Overview

Scheduled for Fall 2026, this course and laboratory expands the 2025 NOLCO laboratory into combined course and lab instruction. Topics include aerodynamic and PMSG electrical control, feedback linearization, sliding-mode control, and super-twisting algorithms for wind turbines.
