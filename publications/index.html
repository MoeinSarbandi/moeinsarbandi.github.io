Journal Articles

2026

  1. WES
    Wind speed estimation using second-order sliding-mode observers: simulation and experimental validation on a floating offshore wind turbine
    M. Sarbandi, M. Viozelange, M. A. Hamida, and F. Plestan
    Wind Energy Science, 2026
    Abs DOI Bib HTML

    This paper introduces constant-gain and adaptive second-order sliding-mode observers for rotor-effective wind-speed estimation in floating offshore wind turbines. The methods are validated in OpenFAST and experimentally on a scaled software-in-the-loop platform.

  2. JPCS
    Control of Floating Offshore Wind Turbines via Control Lyapunov Function-Based Quadratic Programming and Adaptive Super-Twisting
    M. Sarbandi, M. A. Hamida, and F. Plestan
    Journal of Physics: Conference Series, May 2026
    Abs DOI Bib HTML

    This study combines control Lyapunov function-based quadratic programming with an adaptive super-twisting algorithm for power regulation of floating offshore wind turbines in Region III.
