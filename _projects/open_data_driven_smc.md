**Status:** Open for an MSc project or research internship  
**Primary tools:** MATLAB, convex optimization, data-driven control

## Research question

When a system model is unknown, what properties of a finite input-state dataset are sufficient to design and certify a sliding-mode controller? The project will study data informativity for reaching and sliding conditions, with particular attention to limited excitation and bounded measurement noise.

## Possible objectives

- Formulate model-free controller-certification conditions from measured trajectories.
- Compare rank-based and robust optimization conditions under different excitation levels.
- Extend a noise-free baseline to bounded-noise data.
- Validate the results on canonical systems and a wind-energy case study.

## Expected background

Linear systems, introductory nonlinear control, MATLAB, and basic optimization. Prior knowledge of sliding-mode control is helpful but not required.

## Expected output

A reproducible simulation study, a concise theoretical analysis, and a well-documented code package suitable as the foundation of a thesis report or conference paper.
