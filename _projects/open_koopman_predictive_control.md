**Status:** Open for an MSc project or research internship  
**Primary tools:** MATLAB/Simulink, OpenFAST, Python, predictive control

## Research question

Can a lifted linear model learned from nonlinear floating wind-turbine data support accurate and computationally efficient predictive control? The project will construct a Koopman-based model from OpenFAST trajectories and compare its closed-loop performance with conventional linear-model baselines.

## Possible objectives

- Generate and preprocess turbulent-wind simulation data in OpenFAST.
- Select lifting functions or learn a compact lifted representation.
- Identify a control-oriented predictor and assess multi-step accuracy.
- Design a constrained predictive controller for power regulation or load mitigation.
- Evaluate robustness to operating-point changes and platform motion.

## Expected background

State-space control, system identification, and MATLAB or Python. Experience with model predictive control is desirable; OpenFAST experience can be developed during the project.

## Expected output

A validated Koopman predictor, a closed-loop benchmark against a conventional controller, and reusable simulation scripts with clear documentation.
