**Status:** Open for an MSc project or research internship  
**Primary tools:** MATLAB/Simulink, OpenFAST, neural networks, nonlinear control

## Research question

How can online learning improve sliding-mode control when aerodynamic uncertainty changes across operating conditions, while retaining a transparent stability argument? The project will investigate a neural-network-augmented robust controller for a floating offshore wind turbine.

## Possible objectives

- Build a baseline adaptive sliding-mode controller in OpenFAST.
- Train or update a compact neural approximator online for uncertain aerodynamic terms.
- Study the interaction between learning error, robust control gains, and chattering.
- Compare the learning-enhanced design with non-learning adaptive baselines.
- Evaluate power regulation and structural-load indicators under turbulent wind.

## Expected background

Nonlinear control, Lyapunov stability, MATLAB/Simulink, and introductory machine learning. Experience with TensorFlow or PyTorch is useful but not essential.

## Expected output

A stability-supported controller design, reproducible OpenFAST experiments, and a quantitative assessment of when the learning component improves performance.
